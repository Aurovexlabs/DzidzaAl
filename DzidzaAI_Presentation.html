Executive Summary

DzidzaAI is an AI-first educational platform designed to transform how learners acquire knowledge through highly personalized, conversational tutoring and automated content generation. It addresses core challenges in modern education—access, personalization, engagement, and assessment quality—by combining state-of-the-art AI models, robust backend services, and a modern, accessible frontend experience.

Problem: Current education systems are constrained by one-size-fits-all instruction, limited access to quality tutors, and inefficient revision and assessment workflows.

Solution: DzidzaAI delivers individualized learning paths, adaptive tutoring sessions, AI-generated quizzes and flashcards, intelligent study planners, and analytics to guide students and educators.

Target Users: Primary, secondary, tertiary students, professional certification candidates, educators, and institutions seeking scalable, data-driven learning support.

Vision: Empower every learner with a personalized AI tutor that scales across curricula and educational contexts while preserving safety, accuracy, and measurable outcomes.

Key Outcomes

  • Reduced time-to-proficiency through personalized study plans and spaced repetition.
  • Higher engagement via conversational sessions and dynamic content.
  • Administrative insights for educators through analytics and reports.
  • Enterprise-grade security and scalable architecture for institutional adoption.

Platform Overview

What is DzidzaAI?

DzidzaAI is a SaaS platform that blends conversational AI, content generation, and learning science to deliver adaptive education experiences. Core modules include AI Tutor, Chat, Quiz/Exam/Flashcard Generation, Study Planner, Document Processing, and Analytics.

Supported Educational Tiers

  • Primary & Secondary School
  • O-Level & A-Level
  • College & University
  • Professional Certifications

Business Model

Hybrid SaaS and B2B licensing: freemium consumer tiers, subscription-based premium plans for students, and institutional licensing for schools and universities. Additional revenue from professional certification prep bundles and API/white-label integrations.

Problem Statement

Modern learners face systemic issues that impede effective learning:

  • Lack of Personalized Learning: Curricula rarely adapt to individual pace, prior knowledge, or learning preferences.
  • Poor Access to Tutoring: Quality tutoring is unevenly distributed and costly.
  • Ineffective Revision Systems: Students often lack structured, evidence-based revision like spaced repetition.
  • Lack of Academic Organization: Many learners struggle with planning, time management, and tracking progress.
  • Difficulty Generating Study Material: Preparing targeted quizzes, exams, and flashcards is time-consuming for educators.
  • Curriculum Mismatch: Learning materials are frequently out-of-sync with syllabus requirements.
  • Poor Student Engagement: Static content and one-way lecture formats lower motivation.

Solution Overview

DzidzaAI solves these problems with an integrated set of AI-driven features:

  • Conversational AI: Natural language chat and tutoring sessions to help students ask questions, receive explanations, and iterate on concepts.
  • AI Tutoring: Adaptive problem selection and targeted feedback driven by student model and assessment outcomes.
  • Personalized Learning: Onboarding and ongoing behavior-based personalization to tailor content and study plans.
  • AI-Generated Content: Automatic quizzes, exams, flashcards, and summaries aligned to curriculum inputs.
  • Study Planning: Intelligent schedulers and timetables that integrate with personal calendars and retention strategies.
  • Analytics: Dashboards for learners and educators with progress metrics, weak-topic detection, and cohort analyses.

Onboarding & Personalization System

Onboarding Workflow

  1. Landing & initial prompt: short survey capturing education level, curriculum, key subjects, goals, and study availability.
  2. Progressive profiling: follow-up micro-questions based on earlier answers (e.g. preferred learning style, strengths, weak topics).
  3. Autosave & draft persistence: partial answers saved server-side with TTL; draft linked to account on signup.
  4. AI mapping: responses passed through personalization engine to generate an initial study path, prioritized subjects, and suggested micro-goals.
  5. Dashboard personalization: recommended widgets, suggested study sessions, and prioritized content loaded into the user dashboard.

Education-level Personalization

The onboarding captures educational tier (e.g., A-Level, University) and maps to curriculum templates. For example:

  • A-Level: subject modules broken into topics, recommended exam-style practice, time-to-exam scheduling.
  • University: course/module mapping, lecture notes ingestion, research assistance and assignment support.
  • Medical/Professional: case-based practice, high-stakes exam simulation, spaced-retention for critical facts.

AI Personalization Logic

Personalization engine uses a mix of rule-based templates and model-based recommendations:

  • User model tracks mastery per topic, engagement signals, and time availability.
  • AI suggests study cadence and content formats (explainers, flashcards, quizzes) based on learning profile.
  • Adaptive difficulty and content selection updated after each assessment and session.

Autosave Onboarding & Draft Linking

Autosave strategy:

  • Client-side debounce; changes sent to `/api/onboarding/drafts` for anonymous users.
  • Server drafts have TTL and are linked to account at signup when `draftId` is supplied.
  • Conflict resolution favors latest complete draft and preserves partial answers for recovery.

System Architecture

High-Level Overview

DzidzaAI is built as a modular, microservice-capable architecture with a responsive SPA frontend, a Node.js API backend, worker queues for heavy processing, and external AI providers behind an abstraction layer.

Frontend Architecture

  • React + Vite SPA with route-based code-splitting.
  • TanStack Router for declarative routing; Zustand for lightweight app state.
  • Component-driven design system using Tailwind for utility-first styling.
  • Progressive enhancement with offline cache for drafted onboarding content.

Backend Architecture

  • Express.js API layer handling auth, user management, onboarding, and analytics.
  • Modular controllers and services pattern with clear separation: aiService, documentProcessingService, vectorService.
  • Worker processes (BullMQ) for document extraction, vectorization, large-model calls orchestration, and notification delivery.

AI Architecture

  • Provider abstraction layer supporting OpenAI, Gemini, and DeepSeek.
  • Request router with per-request model selection, prompt templates, safety filters, and cost controls.
  • RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) using a vector store and passage retrieval for grounded answers.

Authentication Flow

User -> SPA -> /auth/signup (email) -> server creates user + OTP -> email OTP -> /auth/verify-email -> server issues accessToken (JWT) + refresh cookie Tokens: accessToken (short-lived), refreshToken (httpOnly cookie) Session store for refresh token revocation and audit

API Structure

  • RESTful endpoints under `/api/*` with JSON bodies and OpenAPI-compatible schema.
  • Key modules: `/auth`, `/users`, `/onboarding`, `/quizzes`, `/documents`, `/analytics`.

Routing & State Management

  • Client routing via TanStack Router; guarded routes for onboarding and protected content.
  • State: ephemeral UI state in React, persisted session/user state in Zustand; server-side canonical state in MongoDB.

Worker & Queue Architecture

Heavy tasks (document parsing, vectorization, long-running AI jobs) run in separate workers coordinated via Redis + BullMQ. Workers can autoscale independently.

Storage Architecture

  • Primary database: MongoDB for flexible document schemas.
  • Vector store: managed or self-hosted vector DB (e.g., Pinecone, Milvus, or RedisVector/RedisAI).
  • Object storage: AWS S3 (or compatible) for document storage and user uploads.
  • Cache: Redis for short-lived data, session state, counters, and rate-limiting buckets.

Database Architecture

Core Schemas

User Schema

Key fields: _id, name, email, hashedPassword, roles, educationLevel, subjects[], academicProfile, onboardingCompleted, preferences, createdAt, updatedAt

Onboarding Schema

Fields: draftId, data (JSON), userId|null, completed(bool), expiresAt — TTL index on expiresAt.

Academic Profile

Fields: mastery per topic, selected curriculum, targetExamDate, studyAvailability, preferredLearningStyle.

Subjects / Courses Schema

Fields: subjectId, name, topics[], syllabusMapping, difficultyTags.

Quizzes / Exams Schema

Questions stored with metadata: questionId, stem, options, correctAnswer, rationale, difficulty, tags, vectorEmbedding(optional).

Flashcards Schema

Fields: cardId, front, back, subject, nextDueDate, easeFactor, interval, reviewHistory.

Analytics Schema

Event-driven metrics: user events, session durations, quiz attempts, pass/fail, topic mastery deltas, cohort aggregates, stored as time-series or pre-aggregated collections.

Relationships & Indexing

  • Denormalize frequently accessed profile summaries into the user document for read efficiency.
  • Index on userId, subject, topic, and examDate for query performance.
  • Vector embeddings stored in vector DB; store pointers and minimal metadata in Mongo for join queries.

Scalability Considerations

  • Shard MongoDB by tenant/region for enterprise customers.
  • Use pre-aggregated metrics for analytics dashboards to reduce real-time load.
  • Offload heavy ML embeddings and similarity lookups to vector DB services.

AI System Architecture

Provider Abstraction & Multi-Provider Support

All AI calls are routed through an abstraction layer (`aiService`) that supports providers like OpenAI, Gemini, and DeepSeek. The router includes policy for model selection, failover, and fallback to cheaper or on-prem models.

Failover & Cost Controls

  • Primary model selection based on task (explainers use larger models; short answers use smaller models).
  • Failover chains with configurable retries and fallback providers.
  • Rate-limiting and costing heuristics to avoid runaway bills.

Prompt Engineering & Safety

  • Standardized prompt templates stored centrally; tokenized inputs for performance monitoring.
  • Safety filters and output validators (toxicity, hallucination detection heuristics, source attribution for RAG responses).

Personalization Engine

Maintains per-user model of mastery; updates after quizzes and sessions. Produces recommended next actions and adapts content difficulty.

AI-Generated Content Pipeline

  1. Request for generation (quiz/exam/summary) enters API.
  2. Server-side validation and parameters normalization.
  3. RAG: retrieve supporting context from vector DB.
  4. LLM call to generate draft content.
  5. Validator & degenerate-check: heuristics ensure syntactic correctness and answer keys consistency.
  6. Persist to question bank and surface to UI.

Caching

Cache frequent prompts and generated content (with TTL) in Redis to reduce repeated LLM calls for identical inputs and reduce cost.

Security Architecture

Authentication & Session Management

  • Short-lived access tokens (JWT) for APIs; Refresh token stored as httpOnly, secure cookie.
  • Refresh token rotation and revocation lists for logout and device management.
  • Optional SSO integrations (SAML / OIDC) for institutions.

API Protection & Validation

  • Input validation with Zod/Joi for all endpoints; strict schema enforcement.
  • Rate-limiting per IP and per user for write-heavy endpoints like AI calls.
  • CSRF protection for state-changing requests where cookies are used.

File Upload Security

  • Virus scanning and content-type validation on upload.
  • Presigned S3 uploads to avoid direct server storage limits.
  • Chunked uploads for large files with integrity checks.

AI Output Validation

  • Ground answers with source passages via RAG; provide citation metadata.
  • Run factual-consistency checks and automatic flagging for human review of high-stakes content.

Operational Security

  • Secrets in vault; environment-specific keys; least-privilege IAM for cloud resources.
  • Monitoring and alerting for anomalous usage patterns and abnormal LLM costs.

Component Architecture

User Workflows

First-time Signup

  1. User visits landing → chooses education tier → begins onboarding wizard.
  2. Anonymous autosave drafts to the server while onboarding.
  3. User completes onboarding and chooses to create account → signup with `draftId` links draft.
  4. OTP verification → user is authenticated and onboarded profile attached to account.

Onboarding

Progressive multi-step flow with autosave, preview of recommended plan, and immediate start actions (first study session or quick quiz).

AI Tutoring

  1. User asks question or starts a topic session.
  2. RAG used to fetch contextual sources and LLM generates structured explanations and practice items.
  3. Feedback loop updates mastery model.

Quiz & Flashcard Generation

User selects topic(s) -> chooses format -> system generates questions/flashcards -> user attempts -> results update mastery and schedule.

Document Uploads

User uploads documents -> worker extracts text -> vectorizes chunks -> user can query documents and generate summaries or quiz items from content.

Scalability & Performance

Scaling Strategies

  • API horizontally scalable behind a load balancer; stateless services where possible.
  • Worker fleets scale independently for processing spikes; autoscaling based on queue depth.
  • Redis caching for hot reads (dashboard summaries, recent sessions).
  • Vector DB optimized for approximate nearest neighbor (ANN) queries with sharding as data grows.

Performance Optimizations

  • Cache LLM responses for identical prompts for short TTL to save cost.
  • Use incremental indexing for document ingestion; stream chunks to vector DB.
  • Precompute user summary cards for dashboard to avoid heavy joins on request.
